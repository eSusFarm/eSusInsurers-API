Project Charter: eSusFarm
=========================

Project Title:
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

eSusFarm: Empowering Smallholder Farmers Through Technology

Project Description:
--------------------

eSusFarm is a technology-driven platform designed to empower smallholder
farmers in Africa by providing access to digital advisory services,
financial solutions, market connections, and sustainable farming
practices. The project aims to address challenges such as limited access
to information, market inefficiencies, and climate-related risks faced
by smallholder farmers, ultimately promoting food security and economic
development in rural communities.

Project Objectives:
-------------------

1. Digitize agricultural processes: Implement digital tools and
   solutions to streamline agricultural activities, improve
   productivity, and enhance data-driven decision-making for smallholder
   farmers.
2. Facilitate market access: Connect smallholder farmers with buyers,
   suppliers, financial institutions, and other stakeholders to improve
   market access and create economic opportunities.
3. Promote sustainability: Encourage the adoption of sustainable farming
   practices, such as regenerative agriculture and climate-smart
   techniques, to mitigate environmental impact and enhance resilience
   to climate change.
4. Enhance financial inclusion: Provide access to financial services,
   including microloans, insurance, and mobile payments, to enable
   smallholder farmers to invest in their farms and manage financial
   risks effectively.

Project Scope:
--------------

Inclusions:
~~~~~~~~~~~

-  Development and deployment of the eSusFarm platform, including
   web-based and USSD interfaces.
-  Integration of digital advisory services, financial solutions, market
   connections, and agronomic data collection tools into the platform.
-  Collaboration with mobile network operators, financial institutions,
   agricultural organizations, and government agencies.
-  Pilot implementation in select regions of South Africa, Uganda,
   Eswatini, and Nigeria.

Exclusions:
~~~~~~~~~~~

-  Physical infrastructure development (e.g., farm equipment,
   warehouses).
-  Direct provision of agricultural inputs or commodities.
-  Regulatory or policy changes beyond the project’s control.

Project Deliverables:
---------------------

1. Fully functional eSusFarm platform with user-friendly interfaces for
   farmers, buyers, and other stakeholders.
2. Data collection and analysis tools to monitor agricultural
   activities, market trends, and environmental factors.
3. Partnerships and collaborations established with key stakeholders in
   the agricultural value chain.
4. Pilot implementation reports and evaluation findings to assess the
   platform’s impact on smallholder farmers and the agricultural sector.

Project Timeline:
-----------------

-  Phase 1 (Planning and Development): 6 months
-  Phase 2 (Pilot Implementation): 12 months
-  Phase 3 (Scale-Up and Expansion): Ongoing

Project Risks:
--------------

-  Technological challenges (e.g., connectivity issues, data security
   concerns).
-  Adoption barriers among smallholder farmers (e.g., digital literacy,
   cultural preferences).
-  Regulatory constraints and compliance requirements.
-  Climate-related risks and natural disasters affecting agricultural
   production.

Project Governance:
-------------------

-  Regular project meetings and progress updates.
-  Stakeholder engagement and consultation throughout the project
   lifecycle.
-  Risk management and mitigation strategies.
-  Monitoring and evaluation of project outcomes and impact.

Licensing Strategy:
-------------------
1. Objectives & Alignment with UNICEF Open Source Principles
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
 - **Transparency & Openness:**

        Ensure that all core components—including data governance tools, algorithms, and associated documentation—are openly accessible. This reinforces accountability and supports community review, aligning with UNICEF’s commitment to transparency.

 - **Inclusivity & Farmer Empowerment:**

    Adopt licensing terms that protect farmers’ rights and foster collaborative innovation. The chosen license must enable local adaptation and co-creation while preserving the openness of the core software.

 - **Global Interoperability & Adaptability:**

    Ensure that our licensed software is interoperable with other platforms (e.g., national farmer databases or insurance systems) and can be locally tailored to meet diverse agricultural contexts.

 - **Digital Public Good (DPG) Standards:**
    Meet the DPG Alliance Principles by ensuring that the platform is: Openly licensed: All software and data models are distributed under an open license.Locally adaptable: Solutions can be customized for local contexts.

 - **Interoperable:** 
    Designed for seamless integration with complementary systems.

2. License Selection: Mozilla Public License Version 2.0 (MPL 2.0)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 - **Rationale for MPL 2.0:**

   MPL 2.0 is a copyleft license that:

    **Balances Openness and Flexibility:** It requires modifications to the licensed code to remain open, yet it allows for proprietary components to interface with it as long as the core remains accessible.

    **Encourages Contributions:** It creates a transparent development process where improvements by third parties are shared back with the community.

    **Supports Integration:** MPL 2.0 is well-suited for modular systems like eSusFarm, ensuring that both community-driven innovation and stakeholder-specific customization are possible.

3. Contributor and Intellectual Property (IP) Management
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 - **Contributor License Agreements (CLAs):**

    Require all external contributors to sign a CLA. This ensures that any modifications or enhancements to the core eSusFarm components are clearly licensed under MPL 2.0, protecting both the project and its contributors.

 - **IP Ownership & Data Governance:**

    Farmer-Centric Data Governance: Incorporate clear provisions in the licensing documentation that reinforce farmer control over personal data.Clear Usage Guidelines: Provide detailed documentation on how data is collected, stored, and utilized, and ensure these practices align with international data protection standards (e.g., GDPR).

4. Compliance, Transparency, and Documentation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 - **Open Documentation:**

    Maintain comprehensive documentation that explains:

        The licensing terms of the eSusFarm platform.
        How modifications should be contributed back under MPL 2.0.
        Data governance policies and farmer-centric data rights.

 - **Community Governance Model:**

    Establish a community-led governance board that includes representatives from farmer communities, technical contributors, and key stakeholders (NGOs, governments, and private partners). This board will:

    - Oversee compliance with the licensing strategy.
    - Address disputes related to IP or data governance.
    - Ensure the platform evolves in line with ethical standards and global best practices.

5. Dual Licensing & Revenue Diversification (Optional Extensions)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 - **Core vs. Ancillary Components:**

    - **Core Platform:** Remains under MPL 2.0 to preserve openness and community trust. Ancillary Tools/Services: Consider offering dual licensing for proprietary extensions (e.g., advanced analytics modules or premium compliance certifications) that can be licensed on a subscription basis while keeping the core governance tools open.

 - **Revenue Streams:**

    Leverage licensing fees, consulting services, and revenue from anonymized data monetization as outlined in the broader revenue diversification strategy—while ensuring that all primary tools remain freely accessible as Digital Public Goods.

6. Implementation Roadmap
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
    - **Phase 1:** Policy and Documentation Development

        - Draft clear licensing documentation and user guides.
        - Develop and publish farmer-centric data governance policies.

    - **Phase 2:** Community Engagement & CLA Rollout

        - Engage with the community to finalize Contributor License Agreements.
        - Host workshops to educate stakeholders on the licensing model and data governance.

    - **Phase 3:** Integration and Continuous Improvement

        - Integrate licensing terms into the platform’s release cycle.
        - Monitor compliance, update documentation, and solicit community feedback to continuously refine the licensing strategy.


By adopting this Licensing Strategy, eSusFarm will not only adhere to UNICEF’s open source guidelines but also establish a robust framework that fosters ethical, inclusive, and scalable digital agriculture solutions. This approach reinforces trust with farmers and stakeholders alike while opening avenues for innovation, collaboration, and diversified revenue.
