Privacy Policy
===================
eSusFarm


Effective Date: 20th July 2025
------------------------------
Last Updated: 20th July 2025
------------------------------
This Privacy Policy explains how eSusFarm (“we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data in compliance with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 of Uganda.

1. Legal Basis for Processing
-------------------------------
We process your data based on:
 - Your consent (explicit and informed)
 - Performance of a contract (e.g., insurance or advisory services)
 - Public interest (e.g., agricultural planning or disaster response)
 - Legal obligation under Ugandan law


2. What Data We Collect
----------------------------
We may collect:
 - Identifiable personal data: Name, phone number, national ID, location (GPS or village)
 - Farm-specific data: Farm size, crops grown, soil type, yield history
 - Usage data: Web/USSD activity, feedback logs, device information

3. Why We Collect Your Data
--------------------------------
We use your data to:
 - Provide accurate and tailored agronomic support
 - Deliver financial services such as insurance or credit
 - Communicate updates or service alerts
 - Comply with legal and regulatory reporting
 - Improve our technology and outreach programs


4. How We Protect Your Data
-------------------------------
We apply appropriate security safeguards including:
End-to-end encryption

 - Access control and authentication
 - Staff training on data protection
 - Regular security audits


5. Who We Share Data With
----------------------------
We may share your data with:
 - Authorized partners: agronomists, insurers, telcos, or NGOs working with us.
 - Government authorities: only where legally required.
 - Third-party processors: bound by confidentiality and security agreements.
 - We do not sell your personal data.

6. Your Rights Under Ugandan Law
---------------------------------
As a data subject, you have the right to:
Access your data

 - Rectify inaccuracies
 - Erase your data (unless required by law)
 - Withdraw consent at any time
 - Object to certain data uses
 - File a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO)
 - You may exercise these rights by contacting us at wastson@esusfarm.africa

7. Retention and Deletion
---------------------------
We retain your data only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or as required by law. After this, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

8. International Transfers
---------------------------
Where data is stored or processed outside Uganda, we ensure that the receiving party upholds data protection standards equal to or higher than Uganda’s.

9. Use of Cookies and Analytics
-------------------------------
We use basic cookies and analytics tools to monitor website performance and user experience. You may adjust browser settings to decline cookies.
