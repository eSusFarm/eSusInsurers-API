Do No Harm By Design
======================
eSusFarm

1. Scope & Stakeholders
-----------------------
Primary users: smallholder farmers (often low-income, varying literacy levels) Secondary users: agronomists, insurers, financial institutions, NGOs, and government partners. Geographic scope: Uganda and wider Africa (tapipedia.org).

2. Key Principles
------------------
Beneficence: Aim to improve farmer incomes and resilience. 
**Non‑maleficence**: Prevent inadvertent harm (financial, informational, social). 
**Justice**: Fair access and equitable benefits across user groups. 
**Autonomy**: Empower informed farmer agency and choice. 
**Privacy & Security**: Safeguard data integrity and confidentiality. 
**Transparency & Accountability**: Clear processes and feedback loops. 

3. Risk Areas & Mitigations
---------------------------
  **Risk Area**: Data Privacy & Security
  **Potenatial Harm**: Misuse of sensitive data (e.g., demographics, financial info); risk of breaches via web/USSD platform.
  **Mitigation Strategies**: Use encryption (at rest & in transit), access controls, data minimization. Communicate a clear data privacy policy.

4. Principles in Action
--------------------------
Privacy by Design: Only collect needed data, secure storage, and clear retention policies. 
 - Explainable AI: Use models farmers can trust; offer simple explanations/hyperlinks. 
 - Inclusive UX: USSD-first design with native language options, iconography, and visual aids. 
 - Transparent Insurance Terms: Clearly defined triggers, payouts—explained in plain language. 
 - User Feedback Loop: Channels for farmers to report errors or request clarifications. 
 - Continuous Monitoring: Assess tool impact, review metrics like user uptake, payout fairness, satisfaction. 

5. Implementation Roadmap
---------------------------
Risk Audit & Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) 
 - Secure Infrastructure Setup – encryption, auth, access control 
 - Model & Advice Validation – expert review for regional relevance 
 - Inclusive UX Testing – ensure clarity and usability 
 - Field Pilot with Metrics – collect baseline yield, income, satisfaction 
 - Training Programs – financial, tech, agronomic literacy 
 - Iterative Refinement – update based on feedback and reviews 
 - Governance & Accountability – assign oversight roles, regular audits (formal/informal) 

6. Monitoring & KPIs
---------------------
 - Adoption rates by region/language 
 - Accuracy: match between indexed events and actual crop losses 
 - User satisfaction surveys, issue logs 
 - Equity indicators: uptake across gender, income, areas 
 - Security reports: breaches, resolution timeframes 
 - UX data: error rates, drop-off points in USSD/web flows 

7. Governance & Responsibility
-------------------------------
Define roles (e.g., Data Protection Officer, Agronomic Advisor) 
Schedule Routine Reviews (quarterly)
Commit to public transparency: publish annual reports/summaries about harms and mitigations 

8. Conclusion
----------------
Implementing “Do No Harm” means embedding ethical design across eSusFarm’s system—from data handling to UI, advice, and insurance products. This ensures the platform genuinely supports farmers rather than introducing new burdens or inequalities.
